About Plant Habitat

Plant Habitat is a hydroponics enterprise based in San Fernando, Pampanga. Founded in 2021, it began as a small hydroponic farming project and grew into a provider of produce, systems, materials, greenhouse construction, consultations, and workshops.

We aim to make modern farming more accessible by teaching aspiring growers how to grow food with hydroponic systems.

ATI-accredited Learning Site for Agriculture

What we stand for

  • Promoting modern agriculture
  • Teaching people about hydroponics
  • Helping new growers start their own systems
  • Providing fresh hydroponically grown food
  • Making farming accessible to younger generations
  • Sustainable, resource-efficient farming
